Metric Interpretation Guide

Median Absolute Error is 15.59 hours, while MAE is 251.99 hours. This substantial divergence is driven by the extreme right tail (tickets taking thousands of hours). The typical ticket is predicted within 16 hours.

Our classifier achieved 88.7% Recall with 59.1% Precision. In civic triage, missing a delayed complaint (FN=62) is far worse than screening a routine ticket early (FP=338).

Because only ~20.9% of complaints are long delays, ROC-AUC (0.9262) can look overly optimistic due to large True Negatives. The PR-AUC of 0.7227 substantially outperforms the 0.209 no-skill baseline, demonstrating robust precision across thresholds.

The 124.925-hour cutoff is an empirical 75th percentile derived strictly from 2023 training data. It is an academic baseline, not an official municipal SLA violation.
