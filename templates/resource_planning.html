Simulation Mechanics, Open-Ticket Audit, and Caveats

  • 222 Unresolved/Unclosed Tickets: Computed in Phase 1 & 3 as total_records - (status == 'Closed' & closed_date.notna()). Represents 154 non-Closed status rows plus 68 rows marked 'Closed' but lacking a closing timestamp.
  • 196 Null Closed-Date Records: Strictly rows where closed_date.isna(). This includes 128 non-Closed rows and 68 Closed-with-null rows.
  • 217 Excluded from Supervised Modeling: The 196 null-date rows plus 21 negative-duration entry errors.
  • Distinction: These are historical sample anomalies from 2023–2024, not NYC's live municipal backlog.

Action Space (6 actions): Choose next slot from Illegal Parking, Noise - Residential, HEAT/HOT WATER, Blocked Driveway, Noise - Street/Sidewalk, or Other.

State Tuple (1,764 total bins): (highest_risk_queue, oldest_queue, capacity_tier, backlog_tier, risk_pressure).

Reward Function: r = w_risk * pred_risk + w_wait * norm_wait - w_backlog * risk_backlog - penalties. Zero visibility into post-resolution duration or labels during decision steps.
